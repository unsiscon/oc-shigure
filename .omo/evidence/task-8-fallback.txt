------------------------------------------------------------
  The TUI event bus of opencode 1.18.10 does not deliver session.next.*
  events (verified by an independent event probe during todo 8 recon:
  session.next.tool.called / session.next.shell.* counts = 0 while
  message.part.delta = 54 and session.status = 2 were delivered).
  Runtime-observable states: 待机 (initial) / 思考 (session.status busy) /
  完成 (session.idle -> success transient) / 等待 (question.asked).
  Smoke S3 asserts 思考+完成 (both PASSED); the working semantics are
  covered by unit tests (reducer S01-S15 matrix).
